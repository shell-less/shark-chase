Shark Chase

Choose a side. Turtle: escape the sharks and collect pearls. Shark: hunt the turtles before time runs out. Every turtle you catch adds 5 seconds to the timer. Drag to move, Q to dash, P to pause, keys 1 to 4 (or the bottom buttons) to buy upgrades.

Upgrades

Earn pearls (or turtle catches as the shark) and spend them on upgrades with keys 1 to 4 or the bottom buttons.

Turtle
Flippers: swim 10% faster per level.
Dash: each level makes your dash 10% longer.
Magnet: picks up pearls from further away.
Shield: survives a shark bite, then you get a moment of safety.
Shark
Fins: swim 10% faster per level.
Lunge: longer lunge that recharges sooner.
Jaws: a bigger bite, so turtles are easier to catch.
Stamina: adds 8 seconds to the clock.
